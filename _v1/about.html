I spent my college years in San Angelo, TX, studying acting and technical theater. After leaving college I became a professional Technical Director at a small community theater, then moved to a State University, and finally a renowned Regional Theater in Austin, TX.


Soon after moving to Austin, I hung up the towel on working professionally in technical theater, and decided to get back to my first love of acting...I was always just an "actor acting like a TD" anyway, right? In 2008 I signed with my current agent Heather Collier at Collier Talent Agency. I've become known for gritty, honest, and creative portrayals as a character actor, and am now aggressively pursing an acting career with the same passion that I have always dedicated to my craft.


I enjoy art and photography, am an avid Austin FC and Manchester United football (soccer) fan, and love camping and getting to spend time with my family and dog.
