He was always an actor. His work behind the scenes wasn't a detour... It was the training.

With a foundation forged in both performance and production, Wray spent years building Texas-based theater from the inside out — first as a Technical Director at a community theater, then at a state university, and eventually at one of Austin's most respected regional theater companies. That perspective behind the curtain gave him something rare: a deep, instinctive understanding of how stories are built, and what it takes to make a moment land.

When he stepped in front of the camera and onto the stage full-time, he brought all of it with him. Since signing with Heather Collier at Collier Talent Agency in 2008, Wray has earned a reputation as a character actor of uncommon depth — the kind of performer who makes every scene feel lived-in and true.

His work spans acclaimed Austin stages — earning two B. Iden Payne Awards and multiple Austin Critics Table nominations — to network television sets on Breaking Bad, The Lowdown, Friday Night Lights, and The Night Shift, to feature films under the direction of Robert Rodriguez and Richard Linklater.

Gritty. Grounded. Specific. Wray Crawford delivers the kind of honest, fully-committed performance that directors keep coming back for.
