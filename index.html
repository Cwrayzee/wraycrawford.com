Character Actor in Austin, Texas

Wray Crawford is an Austin-based character actor with three decades of work across film, television, and theater. His television credits include Breaking Bad, Friday Night Lights, and The Night Shift, and he appears in Season 2 of The Lowdown on FX.

On film, he has worked under directors Robert Rodriguez (Spy Kids: All the Time in the World) and Richard Linklater (Bernie). On stage, he is a two-time B. Iden Payne Award winner, with roles ranging from Shelley Levene in Glengarry Glen Ross to Dodge in Buried Child.
